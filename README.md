## Introduction

The Communication Studies curriculum is a complete education in Communication Studies using online materials. This curriculum aims to provides foundational knowledge in human communication theory, contexts, and practical ethical applications.

## Curriculum

### Communication Theory & Foundational Models

Surveys the major models, historical paradigms, and theoretical frameworks that define communication as a human and social science.

[Introduction to Communication Science (Coursera / University of Amsterdam)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/communication) - If you prefer a visual entry point, complete this 4-week MOOC first to build an intuitive grasp of linear, interactional, and transactional models before reading dense academic texts.

[Communication in the Real World: An Introduction to Communication Studies (Open Textbook Library / University of Minnesota Libraries Publishing)](https://open.umn.edu/opentextbooks/textbooks/communication-in-the-real-world-an-introduction-to-communication-studies) - The best comprehensive open-access textbook for this subject, serving as a complete, free alternative to commercial survey books across personal, group, and mediated contexts.

[A First Look at Communication Theory (McGraw-Hill / Em Griffin, Andrew Ledbetter, & Glenn Sparks)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781264296101) - The field's gold-standard theory text; you don't need to read all thirty-three chapters at once, so focus first on the foundational framework in Division One before delving into the theories that align with your interests.

[A First Look at Communication Theory: Conversations with Theorists (afirstlook.com)](https://www.afirstlook.com) - Use this video archive as a companion while working through Griffin's book, watching the original scholars explain and contextualize their models in their own words.

### Public Speaking & Classical Rhetoric

Covers the principles of speech invention, structural organization, audience analysis, and classical rhetorical appeals for effective civic advocacy.

[Stand up, Speak out: The Practice and Ethics of Public Speaking (Open Textbook Library / University of Minnesota Libraries)](https://open.umn.edu/opentextbooks/textbooks/159) - Treat this open textbook as your primary reference manual for outlining, speech structure, audience analysis, and ethical delivery mechanics.

[Introduction to Public Speaking (Coursera / University of Washington)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/public-speaking) - An applied video practicum that works best alongside your reading, especially if you want guided exercises for managing speech anxiety, vocal delivery, and physical pacing.

[Rhetoric: The Art of Persuasive Writing and Public Speaking (Harvard University / edX)](https://pll.harvard.edu/course/rhetoric-art-persuasive-writing-and-public-speaking) - A complementary conceptual deep-dive focusing on classical appeals (ethos, pathos, logos) and civic oratory rather than physical performance mechanics.

### Rhetorical Criticism

Teaches systematic methods for analyzing, deconstructing, and evaluating persuasive symbols, public speeches, and cultural artifacts.

[Rhetorical Criticism: Exploration and Practice (Waveland Press / Sonja K. Foss)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781478634898) - The primary textbook for this subject; after reading the introductory chapters on critical methodology, select two or three specific approaches (such as metaphoric, narrative, or ideological critique) and study their sample analyses closely.

[Rhetorical Criticism (LibreTexts / Scott T. Paynton & Laura K. Hahn)](https://socialsci.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Communication/Introduction_to_Communication/Introduction_to_Communication_(Paynton_and_Hahn)/07%3A_Rhetorical_Criticism) - A concise open-access overview that works well as a quick introductory primer to understand what rhetorical critique accomplishes before opening Foss's comprehensive volume.

### Interpersonal Communication

Examines dyadic communication processes, identity negotiation, relational stages, dialectical tensions, and conflict resolution in personal and professional relationships.

[Interpersonal Communication: A Mindful Approach to Relationships (Milne Open Textbooks / Jason S. Wrench, Narissra M. Punyanunt-Carter, & Katherine S. Thweatt)](https://milneopentextbooks.org/interpersonal-communication-a-mindful-approach-to-relationships/) - The recommended open-access textbook for this topic, providing thorough, free coverage of social perception, relational dialectics, active listening, and conflict management.

[Interpersonal Communication: Everyday Encounters (Cengage / Julia T. Wood)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780357032947) - An established commercial alternative to the Milne text, ideal if you prefer Wood's narrative-driven style and focus on relationship stages, gender, and social diversity.

### Nonverbal Communication

Explores the codes, channels, and social functions of nonverbal messaging, including kinesics, proxemics, haptics, and vocalic expression.

[Nonverbal Communication in Human Interaction (Cengage / Mark L. Knapp, Judith A. Hall, & Terrence G. Horgan)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781133311591) - The definitive scholarly reference for this subject; you don't need to read every empirical citation, but work through the core chapters on kinesics, proxemics, and vocalics for thorough academic depth.

[Nonverbal Communication (LibreTexts / Scott T. Paynton & Laura K. Hahn)](https://socialsci.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Communication/Introduction_to_Communication/Introduction_to_Communication_(Paynton_and_Hahn)/03%3A_Nonverbal_Communication) - A brief, accessible open chapter to read if you want a fast summary of nonverbal codes and their communicative functions without buying Knapp's exhaustive treatise.

### Group & Organizational Communication

Investigates small group dynamics, team decision-making processes, leadership styles, and communication networks within complex organizations.

[Organizational Communication: Theory, Research, and Practice (LibreTexts)](https://socialsci.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Communication/Organizational_Communication/Organizational_Communication_-_Theory_Research_and_Practice) - Work through this open textbook for the organizational half of the subject, paying particular attention to the units on systems theory, organizational culture, and communication networks.

[COMS 120: Small Group Communication (LibreTexts / Victoria Osborne)](https://socialsci.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Communication/Small_Group_Communication/COMS_120%3A_Small_Group_Communication_(Osborne)) - The complementary counterpart to the organizational text, focusing specifically on small team dynamics, member roles, group development stages, and groupthink mitigation.

[Organizational Communication Channel (YouTube / Dr. Alex Lyon)](https://www.youtube.com/@AlexLyonCommunicationCoach) - Watch these short video lectures alongside your reading for concrete workplace illustrations of leadership styles, team conflict, and organizational theory.

### Mass Communication & Media Effects

Studies the historical development of mass media industries and the social scientific theories explaining their psychological and societal effects.

[Mass Communication, Media, and Culture (LibreTexts)](https://socialsci.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Communication/Introduction_to_Communication/Mass_Communication_Media_and_Culture_-_An_Introduction_to_Mass_Communication) - The primary open textbook for tracing the historical development, regulatory structures, and industrial economics of print, broadcast, and digital media.

[Media Effects (SAGE / W. James Potter)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781412964692) - Read this alongside the history text for dedicated coverage of the social-scientific side, focusing on how scholars model agenda-setting, cultivation, priming, and behavioral influence.

[Media Literacy (YouTube / CrashCourse)](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL8dPuuaLjXtM6jSpzb5gMNsx9kdmqBfmY) - A quick, visual 12-part primer that is worth watching first to build an intuitive mental map of media institutions and message framing before tackling the textbooks.

### Critical Media Studies, Semiotics & Representation

Applies semiotic, ideological, and cultural studies frameworks to analyze how media texts construct social reality, identity, and power structures.

[Media Studies 101 (Open Textbook Library / Media Texthack Group)](https://open.umn.edu/opentextbooks/textbooks/media-studies-101) - Start here if critical theory is unfamiliar; its short, accessible chapters introduce semiotics, sign systems, and ideology in straightforward terms.

[Representation: Cultural Representations and Signifying Practices (SAGE / Stuart Hall, Jessica Evans, & Sean Nixon)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781849205634) - The foundational text of cultural studies; read Chapter 1 carefully for Stuart Hall's essential breakdown of semiotic and discursive approaches to cultural meaning.

[Critical Media Studies: An Introduction (Wiley-Blackwell / Brian L. Ott & Robert L. Mack)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781119406129) - A comprehensive analytical toolkit that builds on Hall by providing step-by-step frameworks for applying Marxist, feminist, psychoanalytic, and rhetorical lenses to contemporary media.

### Digital Media & Networked Society

Analyzes computer-mediated communication, networked publics, online identity, platform mechanics, and the societal implications of algorithmic systems.

[Introduction to Communication and Media Studies (Open Textbook Library / J.J. Sylvia IV)](https://open.umn.edu/opentextbooks/textbooks/introduction-to-communication-and-media-studies) - A free, beginner-friendly entry point whose digital culture chapters introduce platform ecosystems, algorithmic curation, and networked participation.

[Digital Media and Society (Polity / Simon Lindgren)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781509541782) - A deeper, more advanced sociological monograph to read after Sylvia's overview, tackling network science, digital surveillance, and data power structures in depth.

### Intercultural Communication

Investigates how culture shapes communicative practices, values, and worldviews, fostering competence across diverse intercultural contexts.

[Intercultural Communication in Contexts (McGraw-Hill / Judith N. Martin & Thomas K. Nakayama)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781260837452) - The field's standard comprehensive text; focus on how its dialectical approach reconciles social-scientific, interpretive, and critical perspectives on culture.

[Exploring Intercultural Communication (LibreTexts / Tom Grothe)](https://socialsci.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Communication/Intercultural_Communication/Exploring_Intercultural_Communication_(Grothe)) - A thorough open-access alternative to Martin and Nakayama, covering cultural value dimensions, taxonomy models, and intercultural competence without cost.

[Intercultural Communication (FutureLearn / Shanghai International Studies University)](https://www.futurelearn.com/courses/intercultural-communication) - An interactive video MOOC that serves as a practical supplement, using real-world scenarios to illustrate cross-cultural conflict resolution and verbal style differences.

### Communication Ethics

Explores the philosophical principles, moral frameworks, and civic responsibilities governing human communication across personal, public, and mediated interactions.

[Ethics in Human Communication (Waveland Press / Richard L. Johannesen, Kathleen S. Valde, & Karen E. Whedbee)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781577665557) - The foundational theoretical book for this subject; read the chapters detailing dialogic ethics, democratic standards, and utilitarian versus duty-based frameworks.

[Media Ethics and Governance (Coursera / University of Amsterdam)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/media-ethics-governance) - A targeted course applying ethical theory to institutional contexts, examining journalism codes, public accountability, and platform governance dilemmas.

[Ethics of Communication (Coursera / University of Notre Dame)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/ethics-of-communication) - A complementary course addressing interpersonal ethics, guiding you through active listening, truth-telling, and moral responsibility in personal and civic relationships.

### Communication Inquiry & Research Literacy

Introduces the foundational ways communication scholars conceptualize inquiry, ask questions, and interpret quantitative, qualitative, and rhetorical evidence.

[Introduction to Communication Research: Becoming a Scholar (GALILEO Open Learning Materials / Lindsey Jo Hand, Erin Ryan, & Karen Sichler)](https://oer.galileo.usg.edu/communication-textbooks/3/) - The primary open textbook for this subject, specifically written to teach communication students how to read, interpret, and evaluate academic journal articles.

[Understanding Communication Research Methods: A Theoretical and Practical Approach (Routledge / Stephen M. Croucher & Daniel Cronn-Mills)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780367623661) - A more comprehensive reference text to consult if you want detailed explanations of how quantitative surveys, qualitative field interviews, and rhetorical critiques are methodologically constructed.

[Social Science Research: Principles, Methods, and Practices (USF Digital Commons / Anol Bhattacherjee)](https://digitalcommons.usf.edu/oa_textbooks/3/) - A concise open reference manual to consult whenever you need a clear, basic explanation of general scientific principles like measurement validity, sampling bias, or hypothesis testing.
