# Resources

A curated collection of practical reference websites, primary-source archives, empirical datasets, and professional portals for self-directed study in Communication Studies.

## Public Address, Speeches & Rhetorical Archives

Collections of historical speeches, transcripts, rhetorical figures, and political campaign media for rhetorical analysis and textual criticism.

- [American Rhetoric (Michael E. Eidenmuller)](https://www.americanrhetoric.com/) - Comprehensive index of over 5,000 full-text, audio, and video speeches, featuring the Top 100 American Speeches and an audio database of classical rhetorical figures.
- [The Living Room Candidate (Museum of the Moving Image)](https://www.livingroomcandidate.org/) - Searchable historical archive of American presidential campaign television commercials from 1952 to the present, complete with transcripts and historical context.
- [Silva Rhetoricae: The Forest of Rhetoric (Dr. Gideon O. Burton / Brigham Young University)](http://rhetoric.byu.edu/) - Authoritative online lexicon defining classical Greek and Latin rhetorical terms, persuasive appeals, and argumentation structures with examples.
- [Voices of Democracy: The U.S. Oratory Project (University of Maryland)](https://voicesofdemocracy.umd.edu/) - Scholarly database of authenticated historical speech texts, comprehensive interpretive essays, and research bibliographies on civic discourse.
- [Archives of Women's Political Communication (Carrie Chapman Catt Center / Iowa State University)](https://awpc.cattcenter.iastate.edu/) - Extensive digital repository of campaign advertisements, political speeches, and public statements by influential women political leaders and activists.
- [C-SPAN Video Library (National Cable Satellite Corporation)](https://www.c-span.org/quickguide/) - Searchable video archive containing over 250,000 hours of congressional sessions, White House press briefings, hearings, and civic programming since 1987.

## Media Research, Audience Trends & Public Opinion Data

Longitudinal survey research, demographic data, and empirical reports tracking digital platform behavior, journalism trends, and public opinion.

- [Pew Research Center: Journalism & Media (Pew Research Center)](https://www.pewresearch.org/journalism/) - Nonpartisan open data and longitudinal reports evaluating newsroom trends, audience news habits, local journalism, and public trust in media.
- [Pew Research Center: Internet & Technology (Pew Research Center)](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/) - Empirical research and survey datasets measuring social media adoption, mobile device habits, algorithmic perception, and digital communications.
- [Reuters Institute Digital News Report (Reuters Institute for the Study of Journalism / University of Oxford)](https://reutersinstitute.politics.ox.ac.uk/digital-news-report) - The world's largest comparative study on global news consumption, digital media economics, news avoidance, and audience attitudes toward generative media.
- [Gallup News: Media & Communications (Gallup)](https://news.gallup.com/topic/category_media.aspx) - Longitudinal polling tracking American confidence in mass media, public views of news bias, and long-term trends in communication channels.

## Broadcast Archives, Fact-Checking & Misinformation Tracking

Broadcast monitoring vaults and systematic claim-verification platforms for analyzing media accuracy, message framing, and political discourse.

- [TV News Archive (Internet Archive)](https://archive.org/details/tv) - Searchable library of more than 3 million broadcast television news programs since 2009, enabling users to explore closed-caption text, trace soundbites, and compare framing across networks.
- [FactCheck.org (Annenberg Public Policy Center / University of Pennsylvania)](https://www.factcheck.org/) - Nonpartisan, nonprofit monitoring resource evaluating the factual accuracy of political ads, presidential speeches, and widely circulated media narratives.
- [PolitiFact (Poynter Institute)](https://www.politifact.com/) - Fact-checking database that rates the veracity of public claims by politicians and news figures using documented primary sourcing and the Truth-O-Meter.

## Media Law, Telecommunications Policy & Freedom of Information

Official regulatory dockets, statutory compendiums, and digital advocacy documentation for examining communication law and information rights.

- [Electronic Comment Filing System - ECFS (Federal Communications Commission)](https://www.fcc.gov/ecfs/) - Public regulatory clearinghouse containing official filings, public comments, and administrative decisions on telecommunications, net neutrality, and broadcast licensing.
- [Commission Documents - EDOCS (Federal Communications Commission)](https://www.fcc.gov/edocs) - Searchable repository of FCC orders, notices of proposed rulemaking, public notices, and official actions dating back to 1996.
- [Open Government Guide (The Reporters Committee for Freedom of the Press)](https://www.rcfp.org/open-government-guide/) - Comprehensive legal compendium outlining open-records laws and open-meetings statutes across all 50 U.S. states and the District of Columbia.
- [Electronic Frontier Foundation (EFF)](https://www.eff.org/) - Civil liberties organization providing legal analysis, white papers, and policy updates regarding digital speech, privacy rights, and platform governance.

## Professional Communication Ethics & Practice Standards

Ethical frameworks, practice standards, and methodological guidelines for interpersonal, public relations, journalistic, and digital research contexts.

- [NCA Resolutions and Statements (National Communication Association)](https://www.natcom.org/resolutions-and-statements) - Official declarations of the primary discipline body, including the NCA Credo for Ethical Communication and credos on free expression in a democratic society.
- [SPJ Code of Ethics (Society of Professional Journalists)](https://www.spj.org/ethicscode.asp) - Benchmark code outlining four pillars of media responsibility: seek truth and report it, minimize harm, act independently, and be accountable and transparent.
- [PRSA Code of Ethics (Public Relations Society of America)](https://www.prsa.org/about/ethics/prsa-code-of-ethics) - Standard ethical framework for strategic communicators, covering core principles of advocacy, honesty, expertise, independence, loyalty, and fairness.
- [AoIR Ethics Guidelines (Association of Internet Researchers)](https://aoir.org/ethics/) - Definitive international guidance document covering ethical decision-making, participant privacy, data scraping, and consent in computational internet research.
- [Poynter Ethics & Standards (The Poynter Institute)](https://www.poynter.org/ethics-standards/) - Practical collection of ethical decision-making models, newsroom guidelines, and practical starter kits for artificial intelligence governance in journalism.

## Open Science & Academic Communication Repositories

Diamond open-access journals and preprint archives facilitating direct discovery of contemporary peer-reviewed research across all communication subdisciplines.

- [SocArXiv: Open Archive for the Social Sciences (Center for Open Science / University of Maryland)](https://socarxiv.org/) - Open-access preprint repository hosting thousands of working papers and pre-publication manuscripts in communication science, sociology, and media studies.
- [International Journal of Communication (USC Annenberg Press)](https://ijoc.org/) - Leading diamond open-access, continuous-publication academic journal covering global communication theory, media policy, cultural studies, and political economy.
