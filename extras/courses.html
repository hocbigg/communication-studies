Great Courses in Communication Studies

A curated directory of standout university OpenCourseWare lectures, high-production MOOCs, and specialized audiovisual courses in Communication Studies that build deep intuition and advanced theoretical models.

Theories of Media, Culture & Digital Society

  • Introduction to Communication Science (Coursera / University of Amsterdam / Dr. Rutger de Graaf)
  • Media Ethics and Governance (Coursera / University of Amsterdam / Dr. Damian Trilling)
  • Media Literacy (YouTube / CrashCourse / Jay Smooth)
  • Applied Social Network Analysis in Python (Coursera / University of Michigan)

Rhetoric, Argumentation & Public Deliberation

  • Rhetoric: The Art of Persuasive Writing and Public Speaking (edX / Harvard University / Prof. James Engell)
  • Introduction to Public Speaking (Coursera / University of Washington / Dr. Matt McGarrity)
  • Think Again: How to Understand Arguments (Coursera / Duke University / Dr. Walter Sinnott-Armstrong & Dr. Ram Neta)
  • Finding Your Professional Voice: Confidence & Impact (Coursera / University of London)

Strategic, Organizational & Crisis Communication

  • Integrated and Strategic Communication Campaigns (Coursera / University of Colorado Boulder)
  • Introduction to Crisis Management and Crisis Communication (Coursera / University of Colorado Boulder / Dr. Burton St. John)
  • Improving Communication Skills (Coursera / Wharton, University of Pennsylvania / Prof. Maurice Schweitzer)
  • Communication Strategies for a Virtual Age (Coursera / University of Toronto)
  • Organizational Communication Channel (YouTube / Dr. Alex Lyon)

Health, Science & Environmental Communication

  • Writing in the Sciences (Coursera / Stanford University / Dr. Kristin Sainani)
  • Science Communication: Communicating Trustworthy Information in the Digital World (Coursera / Erasmus University Rotterdam)
  • Digital Media for Health Outcomes (Coursera / Yale University / Dr. Saad Omer)
  • Communicating Climate Change and Health (Coursera / Yale University / Dr. Connie Roser-Renouf)

Interpersonal, Intercultural & Relational Dynamics

  • Interpersonal Skills (Coursera / Shanghai Jiao Tong University)
  • Intercultural Communication (FutureLearn / Shanghai International Studies University)
  • Communication Skills for Dialoguing Across Difference (edX / CatalystX)
  • Active Listening: Enhancing Communication Skills (Coursera / Specialization)

Communication Research Methods & Analytics

  • Qualitative Research Methods (Coursera / University of Amsterdam / Dr. Gerben Moerman)
  • Quantitative Methods (Coursera / University of Amsterdam / Dr. Annemarie Zand Scholten)
  • Understanding Research Methods (Coursera / SOAS University of London / Dr. J. Simon Rofe)
