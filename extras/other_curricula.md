# Other Curricula

These independently authored roadmaps, disciplinary benchmarks, and open learning pathways provide alternative perspectives, pedagogical sequences, and comparative frameworks to help you structure or diversify your self-directed study in Communication Studies.

- [Learning Outcomes in Communication (LOC) Framework (National Communication Association)](https://www.natcom.org)
- [Media Ecology 101: An Introductory Reading List & Study Guide (Lance Strate / Media Ecology Association)](https://media-ecology.org/Media-Ecology-101)
- [California Transfer Model Curriculum (TMC) in Communication Studies 2.0 (C-ID / ASCCC)](https://c-id.net/)
- [A First Look at Communication Theory: Theoretical Metamodel & Guided Feature Map (Em Griffin, Andrew Ledbetter, & Glenn Sparks)](https://www.afirstlook.com/)
- [Media Studies 101: Open Learning Pathway (Media Texthack Group / BCcampus OpenEd)](https://opentextbc.ca/mediastudies101/)
- [Public Relations & Communication Studies Sequence (Saylor Academy)](https://www.saylor.org/)
