Advanced Topics in Communication Studies

After completing the core foundational curriculum, select 1–2 specialization tracks that align with your research interests or professional goals. Each track provides advanced theoretical frameworks, empirical methodologies, and specialized literature.

  • Rhetorical Theory, Politics & Public Deliberation: Investigates how language, symbols, and arguments construct public knowledge, mobilize political action, and sustain democratic deliberation.
  • Digital Platforms, Networks & Computational Communication: Explores platform architecture, algorithmic governance, digital surveillance, and computational methods for modeling online networks and discourse.
  • Strategic, Organizational & Crisis Communication: Focuses on how discourse, identity, power, and structured messaging coordinate human organizing, stakeholder engagement, and crisis resilience.
  • Health, Science & Environmental Communication: Examines how scientific knowledge, public health interventions, risk perception, and environmental advocacy are framed and disseminated to diverse publics.
  • Critical Cultural Studies & Global Media Systems: Deconstructs media hegemony, transnational information flows, cultural political economy, and ideological representation across global societies.

Rhetorical Theory, Politics & Public Deliberation

Investigates how language, symbols, and arguments construct public knowledge, mobilize political action, and sustain democratic deliberation.

Classical & Contemporary Rhetorical Theory: The Rhetorical Tradition: Readings from Classical Times to the Present (Patricia Bizzell, Bruce Herzberg, & Robin Reames / Bedford/St. Martin's) - Comprehensive primary-source anthology spanning the Sophists and Aristotle to Kenneth Burke, Michel Foucault, and contemporary feminist rhetoric.

Argumentation Theory & Practical Reasoning: The New Rhetoric: A Treatise on Argumentation (Chaïm Perelman & Lucie Olbrechts-Tyteca / University of Notre Dame Press) - Landmark philosophical treatise that resurrected rhetoric as a theory of practical reasoning, audience adherence, and non-formal logic.

Political Communication & Deliberative Democracy: The Structural Transformation of the Public Sphere: An Inquiry into a Category of Bourgeois Society (Jürgen Habermas / MIT Press) - Seminal theoretical treatise on the emergence, structural degradation, and democratic potential of rational-critical public discourse.

Persuasion Science & Cognitive Influence: The SAGE Handbook of Persuasion: Developments in Theory and Practice (James Price Dillard & Lijiang Shen / SAGE) - Advanced research volume surveying cognitive dual-process models, message framing, compliance gaining, and psychological reactance.

Visual Rhetoric & Multimodal Discourse: Reading Images: The Grammar of Visual Design (Gunther Kress & Theo van Leeuwen / Routledge) - Authoritative foundation of multimodal social semiotics, detailing compositional structures, representational meaning, and visual modality.

Digital Platforms, Networks & Computational Communication

Explores platform architecture, algorithmic governance, digital surveillance, and computational methods for modeling online networks and discourse.

Platform Studies & Algorithmic Governance: The Platform Society: Public Values in a Connective World (José van Dijck, Thomas Poell, & Martijn de Waal / Oxford University Press) - Examines how platform mechanisms reshape institutional sectors, public values, and international regulatory sovereignty.

Social Network Analysis & Information Diffusion: Networks, Crowds, and Markets: Reasoning About a Highly Connected World (David Easley & Jon Kleinberg / Cambridge University Press) - Open-access interdisciplinary text exploring graph theory, network centrality, structural holes, and information cascades.

Social Network Analysis & Information Diffusion: Applied Social Network Analysis in Python (Coursera / University of Michigan) - Applied online course teaching network connectivity, centrality metrics, and dynamic graph modeling using NetworkX.

Computational Communication Science: Computational Analysis of Communication: A Practical Approach to the Analysis of Texts and Networks with Python (Wouter van Atteveldt, Damian Trilling, & Carlos Arcila Calderón / Wiley-Blackwell) - Open-access, code-integrated textbook designed for communication researchers covering web scraping, natural language processing, and automated content analysis.

Critical Data Studies & Algorithmic Justice: Algorithms of Oppression: How Search Engines Reinforce Racism (Safiya Umoja Noble / NYU Press) - Groundbreaking inquiry into how commercial search engines and data curation algorithms perpetuate structural racial and gender marginalization.

Critical Data Studies & Algorithmic Justice: The Age of Surveillance Capitalism: The Fight for a Human Future at the New Frontier of Power (Shoshana Zuboff / PublicAffairs) - Monograph analyzing behavioral data extraction, instrumentarian power, and the marketization of human digital behavior.

Strategic, Organizational & Crisis Communication

Focuses on how discourse, identity, power, and structured messaging coordinate human organizing, stakeholder engagement, and crisis resilience.

Organizational Discourse & Sensemaking: Sensemaking in Organizations (Karl E. Weick / SAGE) - Landmark text examining how organizations enact environments, construct shared meaning under ambiguity, and structure workflow retrospectively.

Organizational Discourse & Sensemaking: The SAGE Handbook of Organizational Communication: Advances in Theory, Research, and Methods (Linda L. Putnam & Dennis K. Mumby / SAGE) - Advanced theoretical volume on the Communicative Constitution of Organizations (CCO), workplace power relations, and critical organizational discourse.

Strategic Communication Planning & Campaign Design: Strategic Planning for Public Relations (Ronald D. Smith & Deborah A. Silverman / Routledge) - Systematic professional manual detailing formative research, strategy formulation, tactical execution, and campaign evaluation metrics.

Strategic Communication Planning & Campaign Design: Integrated and Strategic Communication Campaigns (Coursera / University of Colorado Boulder) - Practical course on translating market insights, brand positioning, and channel strategy into integrated campaigns.

Crisis Communication & Situational Reputation Management: Ongoing Crisis Communication: Planning, Managing, and Responding (W. Timothy Coombs / SAGE) - Authoritative text detailing Situational Crisis Communication Theory (SCCT), crisis response postures, and post-crisis image restoration.

Cross-Cultural Workplace Dynamics & Global Leadership: The Culture Map: Breaking Through the Invisible Boundaries of Global Business (Erin Meyer / PublicAffairs) - Practical behavioral framework for decoding communication styles, feedback mechanisms, persuasion norms, and hierarchical authority across multinational teams.

Health, Science & Environmental Communication

Examines how scientific knowledge, public health interventions, risk perception, and environmental advocacy are framed and disseminated to diverse publics.

Health Behavior Campaigns & Message Design: The Routledge Handbook of Health Communication (Teresa L. Thompson & Nancy Grant Harrington / Routledge) - Definitive survey of health message framing, behavioral change theories, digital health interventions, and provider-patient dynamics.

Culture-Centered Health Communication: Communicating Health: A Culture-Centered Approach (Mohan J. Dutta / Polity) - Theoretical and applied framework centering subaltern community voices, cultural narratives, and structural inequalities in health promotion.

Science Communication & Public Engagement: Communicating Science: A Global Perspective (Toss Gascoigne et al. / ANU Press) - Open-access volume documenting institutional policies, professionalization, and public engagement initiatives across thirty-nine countries.

Science Communication & Public Engagement: The Oxford Handbook of the Science of Science Communication (Kathleen Hall Jamieson, Dan M. Kahan, & Dietram A. Scheufele / Oxford University Press) - Research handbook examining cognitive heuristics, cultural cognition, media framing of empirical uncertainty, and science misinformation.

Environmental Rhetoric & Climate Advocacy: Environmental Communication and the Public Sphere (Phaedra C. Pezzullo & Robert Cox / SAGE) - Comprehensive analysis of environmental journalism, climate justice discourses, corporate greenwashing, and public participation in ecological governance.

Critical Cultural Studies & Global Media Systems

Deconstructs media hegemony, transnational information flows, cultural political economy, and ideological representation across global societies.

Political Economy of Media & Cultural Industries: The Cultural Industries (David Hesmondhalgh / SAGE) - Critical analysis of ownership concentration, commercialization, creative labor dynamics, and digital streaming infrastructures.

Political Economy of Media & Cultural Industries: Manufacturing Consent: The Political Economy of the Mass Media (Edward S. Herman & Noam Chomsky / Pantheon Books) - Canonical text detailing the Propaganda Model and structural institutional filters operating in mainstream mass media.

Critical Cultural Theory & Hegemony: Media and Cultural Studies: KeyWorks (Meenakshi Gigi Durham & Douglas M. Kellner / Wiley-Blackwell) - Essential reader compiling foundational texts across the Frankfurt School, British Cultural Studies, feminist media critique, and postcolonial theory.

Global Media Systems & Transnational Flows: Global Communication: Theories, Stakeholders, and Trends (Thomas L. McPhail & Steven Phipps / Wiley-Blackwell) - Comprehensive examination of world-systems theory, electronic colonialism, global news agencies, and transnational digital flows.

Critical Discourse Analysis & Ideological Deconstruction: Critical Discourse Analysis: The Critical Study of Language (Norman Fairclough / Routledge) - Establishes three-dimensional text-and-society analysis to uncover how institutional dominance and ideological hegemony are maintained through language.
